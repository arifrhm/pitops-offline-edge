Engineering Architecture: Offline-First Pit Edge Sync

1. Offline-First Resilience (IndexedDB)

Operates 100% offline at pit depths (>300m) without reliance on live HTTP connections. Forms, previous unit hour meters, and inspection submissions are cached in local client IndexedDB stores.

2. Bandwidth Optimization (Gzip Stream)

Mining wireless mesh (Rajant / Cambium) and private LTE face throughput bottlenecks. Inspection batches are compressed client-side via native gzip compression streams, achieving 65% - 80% payload size reduction.

3. Cryptographic Audit Chain (HMAC-SHA256)

To meet strict KTT / ESDM mining safety audit standards, each inspection record is digitally signed with client-timestamped HMAC-SHA256, guaranteeing that safety forms cannot be backdated or falsified after an incident.

4. Hour Meter & Critical Fault Interlock

Automated anomaly detection identifies hour meter rollbacks and unrealistic jumps. Critical safety failures (brakes, steering, fire suppression) instantly interlock the unit status to GROUNDED_SAFETY_HOLD.
